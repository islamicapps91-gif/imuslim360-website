iMuslim360 Publisher

iMuslim360 Publisher is an internal tool the iMuslim360 team uses to publish content to its own Facebook Page and Instagram account only. It does not offer login to the public and does not collect or store data about Facebook or Instagram users.

If you believe we hold data about you and want it deleted, email iapps26993@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". We will delete it and confirm by email.

The iMuslim360 iPhone app itself collects no personal data; everything stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app. See the privacy policy.
